The Philosophy

Your people.
Your chaos.
Your story.

Tales of Brighteye is built around a simple idea: the most meaningful wedding photographs happen when you are busy living the day. We work quietly, observe closely and step in when a moment deserves a little direction.

Our approach is documentary at heart, cinematic in execution and deeply personal to every couple. From intimate ceremonies to large celebrations, we create photographs and films that feel like you.
